Providers

Sign in once per provider; the sign-in opens in your browser. Harness tokens are kept in the OS keychain (Electron safeStorage) and copied into your environments. Signing in to Puck with GitHub goes through the Puck server, which keeps your GitHub tokens; the app keeps only its Puck session. If the keychain is unavailable, Puck refuses to store tokens rather than fall back to plaintext.
