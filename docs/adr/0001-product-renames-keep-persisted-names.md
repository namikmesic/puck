# Product vocabulary renames never rename persisted or wire names

Puck has renamed several concepts in the product and the docs: the work item became the **ticket**, the instance became the **environment**, the config repo became the **Puck home**, and the "environment" provider kind is the **runner**. Each rename stops at the glossary and at what people and agents read (UI copy, tool descriptions, prompts, comments). The names that are stored or sent stay as they were: ticket ids `itm_<ulid>` and `W-<n>`, the `item.*` protocol ops and the `WorkItem` type, environment ids `env_<ulid>` and the `instance.*` events and files, the `configRepo` store key, and the `environment` provider kind string. Renaming those would need a migration of every user's stores and a protocol version bump for a cosmetic gain, and the stores rule is to dual-read legacy keys, never rewrite them. So a future reader will see `CONTEXT.md` say "ticket" and the daemon say `item.upsert`, and both are correct; the glossary's *Avoid* lists mark the old words as legacy, not as mistakes to fix in the data.
